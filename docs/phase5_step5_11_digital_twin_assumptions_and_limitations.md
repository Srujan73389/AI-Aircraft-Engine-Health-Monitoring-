## Section 2: Complete 40-Variable State Taxonomy (G1–G40)

1. **Measured ($\mathcal{M}$):** Direct pass-through telemetry recorded by physical/simulated sensors in the C-MAPSS dataset.
2. **Derived ($\mathcal{D}$):** Deterministic algebraic combinations of measured telemetry or preprocessed tensor representations.
3. **Predicted ($\mathcal{P}$):** Statistical or neural network inference outputs generated by Phase 4 models.
4. **Simulated ($\mathcal{S}$):** Hypothetical perturbation parameters injected during what-if scenarios.

## Section 3: Core Thermodynamic and Computational Modeling Assumptions

The Digital Twin's software architecture relies on fundamental physical, computational, and mathematical assumptions:

### 3.1 Discrete Quasi-Steady Cycle Replay
- **Assumption:** Each recorded record in the C-MAPSS dataset represents a discrete, quasi-steady snapshot of a completed flight cycle during steady-state cruise or climb conditions.
- **Implication:** The Digital Twin advances in discrete integer increments ($t = 1, 2, 3, \dots, N$). High-frequency continuous-time aerothermal dynamics (e.g., millisecond throttle transients, acoustic pressure pulsations, combustor acoustic modes, rotor torsional vibration) are **unmodeled**.
- **Justification:** C-MAPSS data records single cycle-averaged values per flight. Replaying at the discrete cycle level is mathematically faithful to the recorded sampling frequency.

### 3.2 Lumped Gas-Path Stations
- **Assumption:** Gas turbine components are represented as lumped thermodynamic control volumes adhering to SAE AS681 standard station designations (Station 2: Fan Inlet; Station 24: LPC Outlet; Station 30: HPC Outlet; Station 50: LPT Outlet).
- **Implication:** Internal intra-blade aerothermal flow fields, three-dimensional radial and circumferential temperature profiles, boundary layer separation, and secondary cooling flow recirculation are **unmodeled**.
- **Justification:** Telemetry measurements represent lumped area-averaged static and total values at sensor station taps.

### 3.3 Zero Lookahead and Causal History Buffering
- **Assumption:** At any discrete cycle $t$, the Digital Twin possesses knowledge strictly of the current measurement $x(t)$ and past history $x(1 : t-1)$.
- **Implication:** The internal 30-cycle temporal tensor ($G31$) is constructed via a causal FIFO sliding window. For initial cycles $t \in [1, 29]$, left-edge replication padding of $x(1)$ is utilized to prime the buffer without lookahead leakage.
- **Justification:** Guarantees that online diagnostic and prognostic predictions strictly reflect causal real-world deployment conditions.

### 3.4 Uncoupled Component Deterioration
- **Assumption:** Sensor perturbations and what-if scenarios (thermal stress, sensor bias) are modeled as additive numerical modifications injected into the telemetry pipeline.
- **Implication:** Modifying $T_{30}$ does not retroactively alter the upstream compressor pressure ratio or turbine expansion ratio via closed-loop nonlinear thermodynamic matching.
- **Justification:** In the absence of an integrated high-fidelity Fortran/C-MAPSS numerical simulation binary (which is unobservable and absent in the static C-MAPSS dataset), physical closed-loop aerothermal feedback loops cannot be guessed without introducing unvalidated synthetic artifacts.

## Section 4: Boundary of Representation

To establish absolute clarity for researchers and certification authorities, the boundaries of what the Digital Twin **CAN** represent versus what it **CANNOT** represent are explicitly defined:

### 4.1 What the Digital Twin CAN Represent
1. **Lossless Telemetry Replay & Ingestion:** Replays recorded engine trajectories across FD001–FD004 with 100% exact numerical fidelity ($|\Delta| = 0.0$).
2. **Macroscopic Thermodynamic Stage Checks:** Evaluates macroscopic temperature rise ($\Delta T_{HPC}$), overall pressure ratio ($OPR$), and spool speed ratio ($N_{ratio}$) to detect physical consistency violations.
3. **Data-Driven Diagnostic Tracking:** Tracks unsupervised anomaly scores (Isolation Forest, PCA-SPE) and flags persistent degradation thresholds.
4. **Machine Learning Prognostic Forecasting:** Generates cycle-by-cycle Remaining Useful Life forecasts using Phase 4 champion models (XGBoost and LSTM).
5. **Epistemic Uncertainty Quantification:** Quantifies prognostic disagreement via inter-model spread $|G35 - G36|$.
6. **Controlled What-If Sensitivity Exploration:** Evaluates prognostic sensitivity to hypothetical thermal offsets (simulating EGT margin erosion) and sensor calibration biases.

### 4.2 What the Digital Twin CANNOT Represent
1. **Compressor Stall & Surge Dynamics:** Cannot simulate rotating stall cells, acoustic pressure oscillations, or rapid compressor surge.
2. **Detailed Structural Mechanics & Material Fatigue:** Cannot compute low-cycle fatigue (LCF), high-cycle fatigue (HCF), creep-rupture life, thermal barrier coating (TBC) spallation, or blade crack propagation.
3. **Transient Engine Control & Fuel Metering:** Cannot simulate real-time FADEC closed-loop transient response, variable stator vane (VSV) scheduling, or transient fuel valve dynamics.
4. **Internal Component Wear Laws:** Cannot simulate physical blade tip clearance growth, surface roughness erosion, or foreign object damage (FOD) mechanics from first principles.
5. **Real Engine Control Authority:** Cannot issue real-time actuator commands to fuel pumps, thrust reversers, or bleed valves.

## Section 5: Epistemic Uncertainty and Anomaly Demarcation

A cornerstone of Step 5.11 is the formal demarcation of variables that have no physical ground truth in recorded C-MAPSS data:

### Why G32–G34 and G37–G40 Lack Physical Ground Truth
- **G32 (`anomaly_score_m1`):** Represents an algorithmic score produced by an ensemble of random partitioning trees (Isolation Forest). There is no "physical anomaly sensor" bolted onto a turbofan engine.
- **G33 (`anomaly_score_m2`):** Represents the mathematical squared prediction error ($Q$-statistic) of a PCA subspace projection. It is a mathematical metric, not an avionics measurement.
- **G34 (`anomaly_alert_flag`):** Represents a discrete multi-cycle persistence rule. It is a decision-support heuristic.
- **G37 (`prognostic_spread`):** Represents epistemic disagreement $|RUL_{xgb} - RUL_{lstm}|$ between two distinct neural/tree architectures. There is no physical instrument measuring model disagreement.
- **G38 (`virtual_health_index`):** Represents a proposed mathematical mapping $H(t) \in [0, 1]$ designed to capture normalized degradation. It is not an uncalibrated physical sensor.
- **G39 & G40 (`thermal_stress_offset`, `sensor_bias_delta`):** Represent user-injected hypothetical scenario controls.

### Diagnostic Policy
In strict compliance with aerospace research integrity, **zero artificial ground-truth error metrics (MAE/RMSE) are ever computed for G32–G34 and G37–G40**. Computing artificial accuracy metrics for unobservable mathematical quantities would be scientifically invalid.

## Section 6: What-If Simulation Boundaries & Scope

### 6.1 Thermal Stress Scenarios (G39)
- **Mathematical Form:** Injected into $T_{30}$ via additive offset $\tilde{T}_{30}(t) = T_{30}(t) + \Delta T$.
- **Validity Envelope:** Calibrated to $[-50.0, +50.0]^\circ\text{R}$. Offsets exceeding $\pm 50^\circ\text{R}$ risk pushing normalized inputs beyond the training distribution of Phase 4 models, triggering severe out-of-distribution extrapolation.
- **Physical Interpretation:** Models hot-section degradation, thermal distress, and combustor/turbine exit temperature margin loss.

### 6.2 Sensor Bias Scenarios (G40)
- **Mathematical Form:** Injected into target physical parameter via $\tilde{x}_i(t) = x_i(t) + \Delta b_i$.
- **Validity Envelope:** Calibrated to $[-20.0, +20.0]\text{ psia}$ for pressure transducers ($P_{30}$).
- **Physical Interpretation:** Models sensor calibration drift, transducer aging, or wiring impedance degradation.

### 6.3 Why Accelerated Aging Was Formally Deferred
In Step 5.7 and Step 5.8, accelerated aging execution was formally deferred. The engineering justifications are:
1. **Temporal Discontinuity:** Subsampling recorded cycles ($t_{eff} = \alpha \cdot t$) creates artificial jumps in temporal sequences, violating the 30-cycle temporal autocorrelation assumption of the Phase 4 LSTM.
2. **Unobservable Wear Laws:** Physical component wear laws (Archard wear equation, Paris' law crack growth, Arrhenius thermal oxidation) are not parameterized in the C-MAPSS dataset. Fabricating ad-hoc cycle skip heuristics would introduce unvalidated artifacts into ML predictions.

## Section 7: Scientific & Regulatory Operational Boundaries

### 7.1 Academic Prototype Disclaimer
$$\begin{aligned}
\textbf{DISCLAIMER:} &\quad \text{The Digital Twin software package developed in this project is an analytical} \\
&\quad \text{academic research prototype. It is designed solely for algorithmic benchmarking,} \\
&\quad \text{predictive maintenance research, and digital twin interface demonstration} \\
&\quad \text{using the NASA C-MAPSS synthetic dataset.}
\end{aligned}$$

### 7.2 Non-Certification for Flight Control or FADEC Authority
1. **No DO-178C / DO-254 Compliance:** The software is not developed, tested, or certified under FAA/EASA RTCA DO-178C (Software Considerations in Airborne Systems) or DO-254 (Design Assurance for Airborne Electronic Hardware) Level A/B safety guidelines.
2. **Advisory Decision-Support Only:** Outputs from the Digital Twin (including Remaining Useful Life estimates, anomaly scores, and health indices) are strictly advisory decision-support diagnostics. They must never be used to override certified flight crew instrumentation, flight management systems (FMS), or Full Authority Digital Engine Control (FADEC) units.
3. **Operating Envelope Applicability:** The Digital Twin is calibrated exclusively to the NASA C-MAPSS turbofan dataset specifications. Deployment to operational commercial or military gas turbines would require extensive re-baselining, aerothermal cycle re-matching, high-fidelity transient modeling, and formal regulatory flight test certification.
